# 🌌 Galactic Odyssey

### *Interactive Cosmic Evolution Simulator*

> **Galactic Odyssey** is an interactive visualization exploring the **evolution of the Universe**, from the hot early Universe through cosmic structure and galaxy formation to the formation of the Solar System.
>

Explore major stages of cosmic history.

**🌀 Expansion & Cooling**  
Visualize the evolution of the early Universe.

**🕸️ Cosmic Web**  
Explore large-scale cosmic structures and galaxy formation.
